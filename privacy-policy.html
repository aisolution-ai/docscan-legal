DocScan Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 November 2026 · Developer: MD SHAH PORAN · Contact: contact.scaleroame@gmail.com

The short version. DocScan has no accounts and no servers that store your documents. Scanning, text recognition (OCR), PDF editing, signing and password protection happen on your phone. Your documents leave your phone only when you share, save or print them. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which uses device identifiers as described below. Pro removes ads.

1. Information that stays on your device

The following is created and kept only in DocScan's private storage on your phone. We (the developer) never receive it:

  • Scanned pages, imported PDFs and the PDFs you export.
  • Text recognized from your documents (OCR results) and the text you edit.
  • Signatures you draw, stamps and dates you add.
  • Photos of ID cards you scan. They are treated like any other document: kept only on your phone and never uploaded or shared with ad partners.
  • Document names, folders and app settings.

Passwords you set for PDFs are used once to encrypt the file (AES-256) and are never stored. Android's automatic cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are turned off for DocScan, so your documents are not copied to backups by the app. Uninstalling DocScan deletes all of this data. Deleted documents stay in the in-app Recycle Bin for 30 days unless you empty it sooner.

3. Permissions

  • Camera. Page scanning runs the camera inside Google Play services. The ID card camera asks for camera permission; its photos are processed and stored only on your phone and are never uploaded.
  • No storage or photo permission. You choose files with the Android file picker; DocScan can only read the files you pick.
  • Internet and advertising ID. Used by AdMob and Google Play services for ads and purchases. DocScan never uploads your documents.
  • Notifications are not used for marketing.

4. How we use information and why (legal bases)

We (the developer) do not collect, sell or share your documents, and we do not build profiles. Because no document data reaches us, we cannot read, analyse or disclose it. The limited data processed by the Google services in the app is used as follows. Where the EU/UK GDPR applies, these are the legal bases:

  • Personalised ads (free version): only with your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)), asked through Google's consent tool. You can withdraw consent at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices; withdrawing does not affect processing before it.
  • Non-personalised ads, ad measurement, fraud and abuse prevention: our legitimate interest in funding a free app and keeping ads safe (Art. 6(1)(f)). Non-personalised ads use only contextual information and approximate (country/region-level) location. Even without consent, Google may use cookies or identifiers for frequency capping, aggregated reporting and fraud prevention where the law allows.
  • Purchases and Pro access: necessary to perform the contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • App stability and crash diagnostics by Google Play services: our legitimate interest in a working app (Art. 6(1)(f)).

Retention. We keep no personal data on servers. Documents stay on your phone until you delete them (deleted documents are kept 30 days in the Recycle Bin). Data collected by Google is kept according to Google's own retention policies (see policies.google.com/technologies/retention).

International transfers. Google may process data outside your country, including in the United States. Google relies on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (and UK equivalents) for these transfers.

Controller. MD SHAH PORAN, an independent developer, is responsible for this app (contact below). For personalised advertising, Google acts as an independent controller of the data it collects.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Ad choices: Settings → Ad privacy choices (shown where required by law) lets you change consent at any time. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Remove ads: upgrade to Pro.
  • Delete your data: delete documents in the app (and empty the Recycle Bin) or uninstall the app. There is no account to delete.
  • EU / UK (GDPR): you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict and port your data, to object to processing based on legitimate interests, and to withdraw consent. As we hold no personal data about you on any server, most requests concern data held by Google (myaccount.google.com and adssettings.google.com); write to contact.scaleroame@gmail.com and we will help within one month. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.
  • US state privacy laws (e.g. California CCPA/CPRA, Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah): we do not sell personal information for money. Showing personalised ads through Google may count as "sharing" or "targeted advertising" under these laws. You can opt out at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices ("Do not sell or share my personal information"), or by turning on Global Privacy Control / opting out of ads personalisation in Android settings. We do not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.
  • Other countries: you can exercise the rights your local law gives you by writing to us.

6. Children

DocScan is a general-audience productivity app and is not directed to children. It is not intended for children under 13, or under the age of digital consent in your country (up to 16 in parts of the EU/EEA), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the app in a way that collected their data, contact us and we will help.

7. Security

Documents are kept in app-private storage that other apps cannot read. Exports are written to a temporary folder that is cleared regularly. Password-protected PDFs use AES-256 encryption. No method is perfectly secure; if your phone is rooted or someone else unlocks it, files on it may be accessible.

8. Electronic signatures

Signatures drawn in DocScan are simple electronic signatures. They are stored only on your device. They are valid for many everyday agreements but are not "qualified" signatures and may not be accepted for wills, court filings or other documents that require special formalities.
